MINISTER OF DEFENCE
Marshal-Director Vorn Caelith
A decorated veteran of the Outer Belt Pacification campaigns, Vorn Caelith rose through the 1st Fleet strike command before being elevated to the Ministry following the Y.31 Defence Restructuring Act. He has presided over the largest fleet expansion in UA history, overseeing the commissioning of the Abaia-class carrier programme and the establishment of the Vexsis Forward Operating Base. A proponent of deterrence doctrine, Caelith is credited with zero inter-world armed incidents during his tenure.

ORGANISATIONAL STRUCTURE
Branches & Commands
The Ministry of Defence operates through six primary branches responsible for fleet operations, planetary security, intelligence, logistics, procurement, and the Vexsis forward command.
MOD-FLCOM · BRANCH I
Fleet Command
Commands all commissioned naval vessels across the Regulus system. Maintains the 1st Fleet (Abaia Theatre), 2nd Fleet (Liora Theatre), and 3rd Expeditionary (Vexsis). Coordinates patrol schedules, convoy escort rotations, and force projection exercises. Responsible for fleet readiness certification and tactical doctrine.

MOD-PGC · BRANCH II
Planetary Garrison Command
Administers all ground-based military forces across UA-held worlds. Maintains permanent garrisons on Abaia and Liora and the reinforced expeditionary post on Vexsis. Oversees perimeter security for extraction zones, port authority enforcement, and civil emergency military assistance protocols.

MOD-DIA · BRANCH III
Defence Intelligence Agency
Provides strategic and operational intelligence to MOD command elements and the Office of the High Admiral. Operates sensor networks across the outer belt, monitors non-UA vessel activity in Regulus space, and produces the quarterly Threat Assessment Report. Coordinates with MOIAC on internal security matters.

Logistics & Supply Command

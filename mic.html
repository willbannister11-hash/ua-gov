ORGANISATIONAL STRUCTURE
Departments & Offices
MIC operates through five primary departments responsible for commercial licensing, industrial oversight, resource extraction governance, and inter-planetary trade administration across the Regulus system.
MIC-DCT · DEPT. I
Department of Commerce & Trade
Governs commercial activity across the Regulus system. Issues trade licences, enforces commercial regulations, negotiates inter-world trade agreements, and maintains the Central Commerce Registry. Oversees the Taruscav Sorium Auction process in conjunction with the Office of Resource Licensing.

MIC-DIL · DEPT. II
Department of Industry & Licensing
Administers the Industrial Licensing Registry for all manufacturing, processing, and production facilities across UA-held worlds. Reviews and approves new industrial applications, enforces operational safety standards, and monitors compliance with planetary environmental ordinances.

MIC-DRE · DEPT. III
Department of Resource & Extraction
Manages the licensing, monitoring, and taxation of all resource extraction operations in the Regulus system — including sorium, mineral, and atmospheric extraction. Operates the Taruscav Survey Grid and maintains extraction quota frameworks for each world. Coordinates with the OCR (MOIAC) on newly surveyed sites.

MIC-DFT · DEPT. IV
Department of Freight & Transit
Regulates commercial freight movement between Regulus worlds. Sets and enforces inter-world freight tariff schedules, issues commercial transit permits, and administers the Cargo Manifest Registry. Works in liaison with MOD logistics and MOIAC-DIM on movement protocol compliance.

MIC-ORL · DEPT. V
Office of Resource Licensing & Auction
Administers the quarterly Taruscav Sorium Auction — the primary mechanism by which extraction rights are allocated across the system. Manages the bidder registry, conducts due-diligence reviews, publishes lot specifications, and oversees settlement of awarded contracts. Coordinates with the Department of Resource & Extraction on quota compliance.

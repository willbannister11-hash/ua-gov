MINISTER OF INTERNAL & COLONIAL AFFAIRS
Director-General Valdris Omaen
A career administrator drawn from Abaia's founding colonial corps, Valdris Omaen has served the United Admiralty for over two decades. His tenure has seen the formalization of colonial transit rights, the drafting of the Regulus Settlement Charter, and the integration of Vexsis outpost governance under central UA authority.

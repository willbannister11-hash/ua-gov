AUCTION RULES & REQUIREMENTS
MIC-ORL REGULATIONS · TARUSCAV AUTHORITY CHARTER
RULE 01
Sealed Tender Process
All bids are submitted sealed to MIC-ORL by the closing date. Bids are opened simultaneously at the award ceremony. Highest compliant bid wins. No extensions granted except by Admiralty directive.
RULE 02
Operator Certification
Bidders must hold a valid UA Atmospheric Extraction Licence (Class II or III), have demonstrated extraction capacity, and maintain no outstanding violations with MIC or DOE. New operators require MIC-ORL approval 30 days before cycle close.
RULE 03
Minimum Reserve Pricing
Each lot carries a published opening bid (reserve price) calculated from projected yield multiplied by the current spot rate minus operating cost allowance. Bids below reserve are automatically rejected.
RULE 04
Licence Terms & Renewal
Licence terms run 6–10 standard years depending on zone classification. Renewal applications must be submitted no later than 2 quarters before expiry. Renewal bids are treated as first-round bids with incumbent preference of 5%.
RULE 05
Yield Reporting
Licensees must submit quarterly extraction reports to MIC-ORL and DOE-SPC. Failure to meet 70% of projected annual yield triggers a performance review. Two consecutive shortfalls may result in licence forfeiture and re-auction.
RULE 06
Dispute Resolution
Disputes over awards, yield assessments, or licence violations are adjudicated by the MIC-ORL Disputes Board. Appeals may be escalated to MOIAC-DJC under the Regulus Settlement Charter, Section IV.
